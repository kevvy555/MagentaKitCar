A SMALL CAR WITH A LONG STORY

Built to be different.

In an era of garage-built specials, the Magenta combined familiar British running gear with a shape somewhere between roadster and dune buggy.

“The firm has produced a kit car which will bring sports car stability, handling and performance within easy reach of the do-it-yourself enthusiast.”— LIGHTSPEED PANELS PRESS MATERIAL, 1972
