## Historic and technical sources

- [Original Lightspeed Panels press-release transcription, 10 Aug 1972](https://www.djbarryjohn.co.uk/magenta.php) — MG 1100 donor and factory launch technical figures.
- [Classic Kitcars: Magenta](https://classic-kitcars.com/car_details.php?car_id=30) — launch era, donor vehicle, price and subsequent versions.
- [Hagerty UK, Antony Ingram, 14 Nov 2025](https://www.hagerty.co.uk/articles/magenta-still-going-strong/) — Gordon Diffey's Magenta and reported production of approx. 650 kits.
- [AllCarIndex: Lightspeed Panels / Magenta](https://www.allcarindex.com/brand/united-kingdom/lightspeed-panels-magenta) — marques and model names.
- [Motor-car.net: Magna Sport / Lightspeed Panels](https://motor-car.net/british/item/19428-magna-sport) — dates, approximate quantities and variant background.

**Important:** historic factory specs refer to the original MG 1100-based kit and should not be taken as measurements of the black supplied car or the photographed individual examples. Period production totals are estimates and differ by source.
